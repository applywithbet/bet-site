A job board that applies for you. Import your resume, answer a few questions, and Bet builds your dashboard with 20 jobs a day picked for you. New jobs come in every hour.

Press Apply with autofill and Bet’s Chrome extension fills out the application from your resume. It takes about a minute, and under 10 seconds on some sites. You look it over and submit.

Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, Taleo, SuccessFactors, LinkedIn and company career sites.
