# RowList
Rows grouped on one white panel (the tracker on a phone, a people list): logo, title over one line, a chip on the right.

- `ul.bet-list` (22px radius, a 1px `hairline` ring) of `.bet-row`. Rows are split by 1px lines that start past the logo (66px in).
- `.bet-row-title` (14.5px, 500) over `.bet-row-sub` (12.5px, `muted`). Something happening soon is `.bet-row-sub.bet-new`.
- Clickable rows are links or buttons and fill with `tint` on hover.
