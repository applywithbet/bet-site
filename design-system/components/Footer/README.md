# Footer
The site footer: one line about Bet with its second half in blue, three short link columns, the giant faded wordmark with the octopus, then the copyright and the slogan.

- `.bet-footer-in` (the `band` width, a faded `tile-line` rule on top) > `.bet-footer-top` (`.bet-footer-brand` with `.bet-footer-tagline`, and `.bet-footer-cols` of `.bet-footer-col`), `.bet-footer-mark`, `.bet-footer-bottom`.
- Columns are Product, Company, Legal; the current page's link has `aria-current`. Left-aligned text. Under 760px the tagline goes on top and the three columns share the width.
- The wordmark is ink at 15%, solid at its base and gone at the top. On the site a script fits it ink edge to ink edge.
- "Bet on yourself" is the slogan. No social links that don't exist.
