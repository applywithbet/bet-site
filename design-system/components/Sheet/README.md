# Sheet
A band of content that slides up over whatever is behind it: rounded top, a 1.5px `tile-line` edge, and `shadow-sheet` on the top edge only.

- `.bet-sheet`: `surface`, 28px radius (22px on phones), the band's width (`band`, 1240px).
- On the landing page the demos sheet rises over the hero; on inner pages everything after the first section sits on one sheet.
- The section after a first screen should peek above the fold, so people can tell the page scrolls.
