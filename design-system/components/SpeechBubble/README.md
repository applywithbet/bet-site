# SpeechBubble
What Bet says: a rounded bubble with a tail that points at the octopus.

- `.bet-says` (flex, 12px gap) holds the octopus then `.bet-bubble` (`surface`, 24px radius, a 1.5px drop shadow, tail on the left). `.bet-bubble-right` puts the tail on the right for a bubble left of Bet. `.bet-bubble-tint` is the flat `tint` version on white panels.
- Short lines in `.bet-say` (display, 700, 1.4rem): "bet.", "Okay, bet.", "Got it."
