The short version

Bet uses your information to find you jobs and fill out the applications you choose. We don't sell your personal information, and employers only see it when you apply. You can see, change or delete it at any time.

This policy covers Bet's website, app and Chrome extension.

What we collect

  • What you give us. Your resume and everything in it, your answers to the questions Bet asks when you sign up, your job preferences, and what you add to your tracker, like interviews and rejections.
  • Account details. Your name, email address and login information.
  • Application activity. The jobs you view, save and apply to, and the application forms Bet fills out for you.
  • Technical information. Your device and browser type, IP address, and logs that help us keep Bet working and secure.

How we use it

  • To pick jobs for you and build your dashboard.
  • To fill out the applications you start, with the information you've given us.
  • To keep your tracker up to date and show you people connected to a role.
  • To run, secure and improve Bet, and fix what breaks.
  • To email you about your account and, if you agree, about product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

The Chrome extension

The extension works on application pages when you press Apply with autofill. It reads the form on that page so it can fill it in with your information.

It doesn't track your browsing, read your other tabs, or collect what you type on other sites.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With employers, when you apply. The information in an application goes to the employer through their application site, like Greenhouse, Workday or Lever. Their privacy policies apply to it from there.
  • With service providers. Companies that host Bet, store data and send email for us, under contracts that only let them use it to provide those services.
  • For legal reasons. When the law requires it, or to protect Bet's users, the public or Bet from harm or fraud.
  • If Bet changes hands. If Bet is merged or acquired, your information may move to the new owner, and this policy still applies to it.

Cookies and this website

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers whether you picked the light or dark theme, in your browser's local storage, and nothing else.

To show the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and a smooth-scrolling script from jsDelivr. Like any website you visit, those services see your IP address when it does.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except what the law requires us to keep or what we need to resolve a dispute.

Applications you've already sent stay with the employers you sent them to.

Your choices and rights

You can see and change your profile, resume and tracker in Bet at any time. You can also ask us to:

  • Send you a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your account and your personal information.
  • Stop emailing you product updates.

Depending on where you live, for example California or the EU, local law may give you more rights. We'll honor them, and we won't treat you differently for using them. To make a request, get in touch.

Security and children

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls and limits on who at Bet can see it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us if you spot a problem.

Bet isn't for children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16, and if we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

Changes and contact

We'll update this policy when Bet changes how it handles your information. If a change is significant, we'll tell you before it takes effect, by email or in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions or requests? Get in touch and our legal team will get back to you.
